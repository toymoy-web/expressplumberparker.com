The Parker Factor

Why This Happens Here

Disposals in hard-water homes tend to see more mineral buildup around the seal and splash guard, which can contribute to leaks earlier than in a soft-water market. Otherwise, most disposal failures in Parker are the same jam, seal, and motor issues seen anywhere. Homeowners in nearby Lone Tree deal with the same underlying conditions.

Signs To Watch For

Common Signs You Need This Service

Humming But Not Spinning

Usually a jam against the impeller, sometimes clearable without a full replacement.

Leaking From The Bottom

A worn seal, which usually means replacement rather than repair.

Tripping The Breaker Repeatedly

Motor overload, often from a persistent jam or a failing motor.

Unusually Loud Grinding

Foreign object caught in the chamber or worn impeller blades.

Our Process

How We Diagnose And Fix It

We check for jams first using the manual reset and hex key method, inspect the seal for leaks, and test motor function. Units with a failed motor or seal are replaced rather than patched, since disposal repair parts rarely make sense to source individually. FAQs

Questions About Garbage Disposal Repair

Why does my disposal just hum?

That is almost always a jam against the impeller, often clearable with the reset button and a hex key, we check before recommending replacement.

Is it worth repairing or should I replace it?

A cleared jam is worth fixing, a leaking seal or failed motor usually means replacement makes more sense than a partial repair.

What should never go down a disposal?

Grease, fibrous vegetables like celery, and coffee grounds are common causes of jams and clogs.
