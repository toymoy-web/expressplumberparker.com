The Parker Factor

Why This Happens Here

Afternoon summer thunderstorms in the Palmer Divide hail corridor can drop flash-flood levels of rain quickly, and some of Parker's older subdivisions were built with drainage systems that predate current stormwater standards. A sump pump that has not been tested before storm season is a real risk in exactly the neighborhoods most exposed to that runoff. Homeowners in nearby Highlands Ranch deal with the same underlying conditions.

Signs To Watch For

Common Signs You Need This Service

Pump Runs Constantly Or Not At All

Both point to a failing float switch or worn motor.

Musty Basement Smell

Can indicate the pump is not keeping pace with groundwater intrusion.

Visible Rust Or Age On The Unit

Most sump pumps last 7-10 years, older units are a real failure risk.

No Battery Backup Installed

Colorado storms that cause flooding also frequently cause power outages, the worst time for a pump to lose power.

Our Process

How We Diagnose And Fix It

For repairs, we test the float switch, check valve, and motor to isolate the failure before replacing components. For new installs, we size the pump to basin volume and expected inflow, and recommend battery backup for homes in flood-exposed subdivisions. If the issue turns out to be related, we can also handle Water Mitigation during the same visit.

FAQs

Questions About Emergency Sump Pump Repair

Do I need battery backup if I already have a sump pump?

If your home is in a flood-exposed subdivision, yes, since storms that cause flooding often also cause the power outages that leave a primary pump useless.

How often should a sump pump be tested?

Before storm season each spring at minimum, pouring water into the basin to confirm it activates and drains properly.

How long do sump pumps last?

Most run 7 to 10 years, we can assess remaining life during a service call.
