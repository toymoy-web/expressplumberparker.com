FAQs

Questions From Highlands Ranch

Is Highlands Ranch a city or part of Douglas County?

It is an unincorporated Census-designated place, the largest in Colorado, governed as part of Douglas County rather than as its own municipality.

Which villages have older plumbing?

Northridge and Eastridge are among the older sections, with original supply lines now over three decades old in some homes.

How far is Highlands Ranch from Parker?

About 11 to 12 miles west across County Line Road, roughly a 15 to 20 minute drive for our dispatched trucks.
