The Parker Factor

Why This Happens Here

Most tank water heaters run 10 to 15 years before failure risk climbs sharply, and Parker's mineral-heavy water tends to push that timeline earlier for units that were never flushed. Once a tank starts leaking from the bottom seam, that is corrosion from the inside out, and repair is not an option at that point. Homeowners in nearby Centennial deal with the same underlying conditions.

Signs To Watch For

Common Signs You Need This Service

Rusty Or Discolored Water

Usually internal tank corrosion, a sign the tank itself is failing, not a fixable component.

Leaking From The Bottom Seam

Once the tank shell corrodes through, replacement is the only real fix.

Unit Older Than 12 Years

Even without visible failure, a unit this age is a matter of when, not if.

Can't Keep Up With Demand

A growing household often outpaces what an older, smaller tank was ever sized for.

Our Process

How We Diagnose And Fix It

We inspect existing venting, gas or electrical service, and supply lines, then size and install the replacement to match actual household demand. Every installation includes a full pressure and leak test, and where code requires it, an expansion tank on the cold supply line. If the issue turns out to be related, we can also handle Emergency Water Heater Repair during the same visit.
