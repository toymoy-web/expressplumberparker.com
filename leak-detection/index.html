The Parker Factor

Why This Happens Here

Parker's bentonite-rich clay soil moves with moisture, expanding when wet and contracting when dry. That movement stresses underground pipe joints over time, and it means a leak here is often the result of gradual soil-driven stress rather than a single random failure, which is part of why electronic detection matters more than a guess-and-dig approach. Homeowners in nearby Highlands Ranch deal with the same underlying conditions.

Signs To Watch For

Common Signs You Need This Service

Unexplained Rise In Water Bill

Often the first sign of a leak that has not become visible yet.

Damp Or Unusually Green Patch In The Yard

A patch that stays wet or green regardless of weather often sits over an underground leak.

Hissing Sound Near A Slab Or Wall

Can indicate a pressurized line leaking behind a finished surface.

Water Meter Moving With Everything Off

A reliable sign of an active leak somewhere in the system.

FAQs

Questions About Leak Detection

Can you find a leak without digging up my yard?

Yes, electronic tracing pinpoints the location first so any digging is targeted rather than exploratory.

My water bill jumped but I don't see any water, could it still be a leak?

Yes, underground and slab leaks often show up on the bill well before they become visible.

How accurate is leak detection?

Electronic tracing is accurate to within a small radius in most cases, which keeps repair excavation minimal.
