The Problem We See Most In Centennial

The Problem We See Most In Centennial

Water heater failure in original, never-replaced units is the single most common call from Centennial homeowners. A lot of the western neighborhoods were built decades before Centennial's newer growth near the Inverness business corridor, and original 1980s water heaters simply were not designed to last this long, especially running on the same Front Range hard water Parker deals with. Foundation-crack water intrusion also shows up more here than in newer Douglas County subdivisions, since older slab-on-grade construction has had more decades to settle and shift.
