About The Area

Where Old Town Sits

Old Town is the area around Parker's historic Mainstreet. Homes.com lists a small neighborhood of about 340 homes with a median build year of 1984, which is older than the Parker median of 2000 that the same site reports, and notes a high share of renters and a Walk Score of 86. Single-family homes there average about 1,500 square feet, with townhomes and smaller one and two-bedroom homes in the mix. Town of Parker annexation records include an Olde Towne property alongside the original incorporation.

Downtown itself is changing. Axios reported in April 2026 that more than $400 million is expected to flow into downtown Parker through 2030, including mixed-use projects with hundreds of new apartments along Mainstreet.

Plumbing Notes

Residential Calls And Commercial Neighbors

Compact homes and townhomes in Old Town tend to produce the everyday calls: slow drains, running toilets, dripping faucets and water heaters that no longer keep up. We start with drain cleaning or toilet repair where those fit, and look at the line itself before suggesting anything larger. If you rent, tell your landlord first, since lease terms usually decide who authorizes repairs.

Next door, Mainstreet restaurants and shops have their own needs. Kitchens need grease management, and a blocked line in a mixed-use building can affect several tenants. We provide commercial plumbing and grease trap cleaning for downtown businesses as well as service for the homes around them.

Do you serve businesses on Mainstreet?

Yes. We handle commercial plumbing and grease trap cleaning for Parker restaurants and shops, as well as residential service nearby.

I rent in Old Town. Who should I call?

Your landlord or property manager first, because your lease usually decides who authorizes repairs. If you are authorized to call a plumber, we can help.
