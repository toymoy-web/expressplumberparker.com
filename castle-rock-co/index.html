Winter In Castle Rock

Winter In Castle Rock

Every winter brings a predictable run of burst and frozen exterior hose bibs across Castle Rock, particularly in homes with exposed or underinsulated exterior lines. Colorado's freeze-thaw cycling is a real seasonal driver here, the same pattern we account for in Parker.

Summer In Castle Rock

Summer In Castle Rock

Once the weather warms, the call volume shifts toward sediment-clogged water heaters in the newer subdivisions, where hard water sourced from the same Denver Basin aquifer system Parker draws on accelerates scale buildup. Expansive soil conditions documented across the I-25 corridor also mean slab and underground leak calls run steady through the summer months. Homes here share the same regional pressures we plan around in Centennial and across the rest of our Douglas County coverage.
