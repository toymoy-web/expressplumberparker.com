The Parker Factor

Why This Happens Here

Parker sits inside the Palmer Divide hail corridor, an area the National Weather Service has documented as producing some of the highest large-hail report density per square mile in Colorado. Summer afternoon storms can drop flash-flood levels of rain in under an hour, and Cherry Creek and its tributary Sulphur Gulch can overwhelm stormwater systems built before current standards, particularly in subdivisions developed during the 1990s and 2000s growth boom. Underneath all of that sits bentonite-rich expansive clay, which swells when wet and contracts when dry. That movement stresses foundations and underground pipe joints year-round, and it is a common contributor to basement crack infiltration that has nothing to do with the weather at all. Homeowners in nearby Highlands Ranch deal with the same underlying conditions.

Signs To Watch For

Common Signs You Need This Service

Water Stains On Drywall Or Ceilings

Discoloration that spreads or darkens after a storm usually means water is still moving behind the wall.

Musty Basement Odor

A persistent damp smell, especially after heavy rain, often points to slow seepage through a foundation crack.

Warped Or Buckled Flooring

Laminate and hardwood that lifts at the seams has usually absorbed standing water from below.

Visible Foundation Cracks

Cracks that widen after wet-dry cycles are a signature sign of expansive clay movement pushing on the slab.

Our Process

How We Diagnose And Fix It

We start with a moisture map using a penetrating moisture meter across drywall, subfloor, and framing, then trace the water back to its source, whether that is a failed supply line, a foundation crack from clay movement, or storm intrusion through a window well or grade slope. Extraction uses commercial-grade pumps and wet vacuums, followed by structural drying with air movers and dehumidifiers set to the actual moisture readings, not a fixed schedule. We document every reading for your insurance claim. If the issue turns out to be related, we can also handle Water Mitigation during the same visit.

What To Do In The First Hour

If you are standing in water right now, shut off the water source if you can safely reach it, move anything electrical off the floor, and call. Do not run a shop vac on standing water near an outlet. The first hour matters more for stopping the spread of the damage than for the cleanup itself, and a technician on the phone can walk you through safe steps while a truck is on the way.

FAQs

Questions About Emergency Water Damage Restoration

Do you work directly with my insurance company?

We document the damage, cause, and drying process in a form insurers accept, and can speak with your adjuster, but the claim itself is filed by you or your agent.

How long does drying take?

Most residential jobs run 2 to 4 days of equipment time, checked daily against moisture readings rather than a fixed calendar.

Is the water damage from the recent storm covered?

That depends on your policy and whether the water came from a covered source like a supply line versus surface flooding, which usually needs a separate rider.
